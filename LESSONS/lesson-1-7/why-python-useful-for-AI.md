## The three steps of an automation script:
Get data (from a file, database, or online service)
Process it (change, filter, calculate)
Act on the result (send an email, update a record, rename files)
* Once the instructions are written, the computer can run them again and again, even while you sleep.

*** AUTOMATION STEPS ***
* Reads the folder of files → Step 1: Get
* Renames each one → Step 2: Process
* Saves them → Step 3: Act
